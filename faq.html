Everything you need to know about our single-farm milk, lactose-free options, takeaway travel limits, and party catering bookings.

Popular Topics:
🌱 Do you have dairy-free and 100% vegan options?

Yes! We offer a dedicated lineup of plant-based creations. Our cold-pressed fruit sorbettos (like Ratnagiri Alphonso Mango and Wild Black Raspberry) are 100% dairy-free, churned purely with fresh fruit and organic cane sugar.

We also craft creamy non-dairy gelato using virgin coconut cream and oat milk for chocolate and matcha lovers.

🥜 How do you handle nut allergies and cross-contamination?

We take allergies very seriously. Nut-containing flavours (such as Bronte Pistachio and Roasted Pecan) are stored in segregated counter wells away from nut-free churns.

If you alert our scooper to a severe nut allergy, they will wash their hands, sanitize a fresh stainless steel scoop, and serve you directly from an unopened back-up batch tub.

🧪 Do you use artificial colors, food gels, or high-fructose corn syrup?

Never. Zero synthetic food dyes (no Red-40 or Yellow-5), no preservative gums, and zero corn syrup. Our vibrant colors come directly from real ingredients: deep purple from mountain blackberries, rich emerald from roasted Sicilian pistachios, and pale gold from bourbon vanilla beans.

🌾 Are there gluten-free cone options?

Yes! All our standard paper cups are 100% gluten-free. For those who want the full cone experience, we offer crisp waffle cones baked from certified gluten-free almond and brown rice flour (+ $0.75 upgrade).

🥄 Can I sample flavours before choosing my scoop?

Always! We encourage curiosity. Our scoop baristas will happily provide up to three complimentary wooden sample spoons so you can discover your exact sweet spot before placing your order.

🧇 Are your waffle cones really baked in-house?

Every 45 minutes! We pour fresh batter onto cast-iron griddles right in front of the counter. As soon as the waffle turns golden brown, we roll it around a wooden mandrel and seal the bottom tip with melted Belgian chocolate.

🌙 What are your parlour opening and late-night closing hours?

We are open Monday to Thursday from 11:00 AM to 10:30 PM. On Friday, Saturday, and Sunday, our counter remains open late until 11:30 PM for our famous Midnight Waffle Hour!

📦 How long do insulated takeaway tubs stay frozen during travel?

All pints, 2L quarts, and 5L tubs are packed in double-walled thermal containers with reusable eco-chill ice blankets. They are guaranteed to stay rock-solid for up to 3 hours travel time at room temperature.

🧊 How should I store leftover gelato at home?

Keep your tub in the coldest section of your freezer (usually at the back, away from the door). Reseal the snap-lock lid tightly. For the softest texture, let the tub sit on your kitchen counter for 3 to 5 minutes before scooping. Keeps fresh for up to 4 weeks!

🎉 How does hiring the Vintage Mobile Scoop Cart work?

Our vintage pastel scoop wagon is driven directly to your wedding lawn, villa, or banquet hall. It includes 2 hours of live scooping service with 2 uniformed baristas, 6 curated artisan flavours, and freshly baked waffle cones rolled on-site. The cart needs zero electrical outlets!

📅 How far in advance do I need to book party tubs or catering?

For 5L Drop & Go tubs, we only need 24 to 48 hours notice. For large events requiring our Vintage Cart with scooper staff, we recommend locking in your date 1 to 2 weeks in advance via our party catering page.
