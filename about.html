Methodology

The 6-Step Cold-Craft Lifecycle

From the early morning grass pastures to the final frosted counter dip.

01

A2 Whole Milk Pasteurization

Arrives raw at 6:30 AM from mountain cows. Gently batch-pasteurized at 65°C for 30 minutes to preserve delicate proteins without caramelizing natural lactose.

Stage 1 • Gentle Heat
02

12-Hour Cold Maturation

The base rests in chilled aging vats at 4°C overnight. This enables milk proteins to fully hydrate and bind with cocoa fats for an ultra-dense, non-icy mouthfeel.

Stage 2 • 4°C Resting
03

Slow Vertical Churning

Spun in traditional Italian batch freezers. Unlike industrial factory ice cream that pumps 100% air, our blades fold gently, incorporating under 20% natural overrun.

Stage 3 • Sub-Zero Spin
04

Hand-Ribbon Inclusions

As the gelato exits the spigot, our chefs fold in warm Belgian chocolate ribbons, Bronte pistachio praline, and fresh berry preserves by hand using spatulas.

Stage 4 • Manual Swirl
05

-35°C Shock Freezing

Tubs enter a blast freezer for exactly 15 minutes. This locks the micro-crystals instantaneously, preventing ice jaggedness and preserving pure silkiness.

Stage 5 • Blast Lock
06

-12°C Tempered Pozzetti Wells

Held in sunken Italian Pozzetti wells shielded from air and light. Served slightly warmer than commercial ice cream so flavour explodes on your tongue immediately.

Stage 6 • Ready to Scoop
