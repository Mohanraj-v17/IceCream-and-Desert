Catering & Bulk FAQs

How far in advance do I need to book party tubs or the mobile cart?

For 5L Drop & Go tubs, we require a minimum of 24 to 48 hours notice. For the Vintage Mobile Scoop Cart with uniformed staff, we recommend booking 1 to 2 weeks in advance to lock in your date.

Do we need to return the cooler boxes after the party?

No! Our 5L insulated thermal boxes and cooling pads are 100% recyclable and yours to keep. If you hire our DIY warm sauce dispensers or stainless steel scoops, our delivery agent will pick them up the next morning.

What happens if there are leftover scoops in the 5L tub?

All our 5L tubs come with resealable snap-lock lids that fit straight into a standard domestic freezer shelf so you can enjoy leftovers for the next 4 weeks!
