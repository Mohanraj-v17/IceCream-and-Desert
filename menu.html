Got Questions?

Menu & Tasting FAQs

Can I sample flavours before choosing my scoop?

Absolutely! Our scoopers provide complimentary wooden sample spoons for up to three flavours so you can discover your exact match before ordering.

Do your sundaes come with hot or cold fudge?

All our chocolate and salted caramel drizzles are kept at a constant 45°C in heated copper pots, poured warm over cold scoops for the classic hot-cold dessert sensation.

How long do takeaway tubs stay frozen during transport?

Our insulated chill packs and double-walled containers keep pints and party tubs completely frozen for up to 3 hours travel time at room temperature.
