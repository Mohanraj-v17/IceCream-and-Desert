Pricing & Billing FAQs

Are taxes included in the menu price?

Yes, all listed prices are inclusive of all applicable local dining taxes. What you see on the board is what you pay at the counter.

Is there an extra charge for dairy-free or vegan scoops?

No! We believe dessert happiness should be equal. All plant-based and cold-pressed fruit sorbettos are priced exactly the same as dairy scoops ($3.50 - $4.00).

Do party tubs require a security deposit for the cooler box?

Our 5L insulated chill boxes are complimentary and 100% recyclable. No security deposit is collected unless you request a stainless steel commercial ice cream cart.
