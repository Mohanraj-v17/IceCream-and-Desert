Deconstructed Formula

The Anatomy of a Perfect Sundae

Every goblet follows an exact 4-layer textural balance designed to stay frosty and rich.

01

The Warm Foundation

Oven-fresh fudgy brownie, salted butter waffle disc, or torched banana resting at 40°C.

02

Sub-Zero Core

Two dense gelato scoops (-12°C) forming an impenetrable thermal barrier against melting.

03

Thermal Shock Lava

45°C warm chocolate or caramel drizzle creating crisp micro-crust shards on contact.

04

The Crown & Crunch

Chantilly cream cloud, roasted nut praline shards, and a stem-on maraschino cherry.
