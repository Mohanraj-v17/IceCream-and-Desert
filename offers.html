Can I combine multiple promo codes on one purchase?

Promo codes cannot be stacked together on a single bill, but you can always combine a voucher code with our free loyalty stamp redemption.

Are weekly deals like Twin Tuesday available for takeaway?

Yes! Twin Tuesday and student discounts apply to both dine-in waffle cones and takeaway cups. Midnight Waffle Hour perks apply to all counter orders placed between 9:30 PM and 11:30 PM.

Do promo vouchers have an expiration date?

The vouchers shown above (SWIRL20, WAFFLEUP, PARTY5) are refreshed monthly and remain active throughout the current promotional season.
